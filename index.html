How to use this library

Three loops. Do them in order, once per topic, before you consider it practised.

1
Explore

Drag sliders, click cells, watch the diagram respond. The visual is the teaching; the text is backup.

2
Predict

Every lesson forces a prediction before the answer. If you're wrong, you'll see exactly which trap you walked into.

3
Practise

Switch to Independent or Timed mode. No hints, no peeking. Reset stats, retry the ones you missed.
