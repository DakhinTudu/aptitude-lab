Solution

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall. Read after Practice — then race the timed set.

  • Use

    Unit method

    a:b = parts. One part = total ÷ (a+b). Scale once — don't cross-multiply thrice.

  • Trap

    Chained ratios

    A:B and B:C need a common B (LCM). Blindly writing A:B:C from raw numbers is wrong.

  • Check

    Coins / mixture

    Value = Σ (count × denomination). Check units match before dividing.

  • Speed

    Direct vs inverse

    xy = k (inverse) · x/y = k (direct). One product, one quotient — name it first.
