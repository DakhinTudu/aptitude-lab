Module 9 · Replacement & applications

Replacement, ratio change, income-savings, work

Five more families — each a formula card with a quick check you can do in the exam hall.

Replacement

Remove x L from V L of pure liquid, top up with water, repeat n times:

pure_left = V × (1 − x/V)n

Test: V=80, x=8, n=2 → 80 × 0.9² = 64.8 L.

Ratio change

A:B = a:b, A changes by +p%, B by +q%. New ratio:

a·(1 + p/100) : b·(1 + q/100)

Test: 3:4, +20%, −10% → 3.6 : 3.6 = 1 : 1.

Income / exp / savings

Given I:exp = a:b and I':exp' = c:d, plus a savings figure, solve for I.

Savings = I·(1 − b/a) − adjust for change

Test: I:exp = 5:4, savings = ₹10,000 → I = 50,000, exp = 40,000.

Ratio of work / efficiency

If efficiencies are a:b, times are inverse: b:a. Wages are a:b.

Efficiency ∝ 1 / time

Test: A:B efficiency 3:2 → time A:B = 2:3. If B takes 18d, A takes 12d.

Ratio of areas / volumes

If dimensions are in ratio a:b:

Area a²:b² · Volume a³:b³

Test: two spheres with radii 2:3 → surface area 4:9, volume 8:27.

Compound ratio

Duplicate (a:b)² = a²:b². Triplicate = a³:b³. Sub-duplicate = √a:√b. Sub-triplicate = ∛a:∛b.

Test: duplicate of 2:3 is 4:9. Sub-duplicate of 16:25 is 4:5.

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall.

Use

Unit method

a:b = parts. One part = total ÷ (a+b). Scale once — don't cross-multiply thrice.

Trap

Chained ratios

A:B and B:C need common B (LCM). Raw concatenation is wrong.

Check

Coins / mixture

Value = Σ (count × denomination). Check units match before dividing.

Note

Partnership

Profit ratio = investment × time. Never just investment.
