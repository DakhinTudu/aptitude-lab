3. Core Exam Rules & Fast Shortcuts

Unit conversions, clearance formulas, and relative speed principles.

The 5/18 Unit Conversion

Always harmonize units before calculating:

$S_{\text{m/s}} = S_{\text{km/h}} \times \frac{5}{18}$  |  $S_{\text{km/h}} = S_{\text{m/s}} \times \frac{18}{5}$
  • 36 km/h = 10 m/s  •  54 km/h = 15 m/s
  • 72 km/h = 20 m/s  •  90 km/h = 25 m/s

The Clearance Distance Law

The distance depends on whether the object has physical length:

  • Pole / Tree / Person (0 length): $D = L_{\text{train}}$
  • Bridge / Platform / Tunnel: $D = L_{\text{train}} + L_{\text{bridge}}$
  • Two Trains Crossing: $D = L_1 + L_2$

Relative Speed Directions

When both objects are in motion:

  • Opposite Directions ($\to \leftarrow$): $S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 + S_2$
  • Same Direction ($\to \to$): $S_{\text{rel}} = |S_1 - S_2|$
  • Man in another train: $D = L_{\text{passing train}}$ only!
