3. Core Exam Rules & Fast Shortcuts

Relative turns, Pythagorean triples, and shadow rules.

The Facing-South Trap

When a person walks South, their left and right are opposite to yours:

  • Facing North $\implies$ Left = West, Right = East
  • Facing South $\implies$ Left = East, Right = West
  • Facing East $\implies$ Left = North, Right = South
  • Facing West $\implies$ Left = South, Right = North

Standard Pythagorean Triples

Spot right triangles instantly to avoid long square-root calculations:

$3-4-5$  |  $5-12-13$  |  $7-24-25$  |  $8-15-17$

Multiples also hold: $(6, 8, 10)$, $(9, 12, 15)$, $(10, 24, 26)$, etc.

Sun & Shadow Rules

Shadow always falls opposite to the sun:

  • Morning (Sunrise in East): All shadows fall towards West.
  • Evening (Sunset in West): All shadows fall towards East.
  • At 12:00 Noon: No shadow (sun directly overhead).
