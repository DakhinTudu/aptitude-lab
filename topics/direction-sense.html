Rules that save time

Pythagorean triples

Spot these instantly: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 7-24-25, 8-15-17. Multiples also work.

Shadow rules

Morning sun → shadows fall West. Evening sun → shadows fall East. If the shadow is to someone's right, that direction is East (morning) or West (evening).

Opposite rule

"A is NE of B" implies "B is SW of A." Both axes flip: N↔S, E↔W.
