Understand: absolute vs percentage — predict first

Bigger rupee jump ≠ higher growth. Absolute change and % change are different questions. Predict first — then see both bars. Mind rule: always ask “of what base?”

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall. Read after Practice — then race the timed set.

  • Use

    Anchor the base

    "A is x% more than B" → divide by B. "of" → that noun is the base.

  • Trap

    Wrong denominator

    % of total ≠ % of another company. Say the base out loud.

  • Check

    YoY

    (new−old)/old × 100 — never /new.

  • Speed

    Approx first

    Eliminate 2 options with rough %, then compute precisely once.
