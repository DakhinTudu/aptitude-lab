3. Core Exam Rules & Fast Shortcuts

The three pillars, successive discounts, and equal SP rules.

The Three Price Pillars

Always verify which base you are calculating on:

  • Cost Price (CP): Markup % and Profit/Loss % are calculated on CP.
  • Marked Price (MP): Discount % is strictly calculated on MP.
  • Selling Price (SP): The cash exchanged ($SP = MP - \text{Discount}$).

Successive Discount Formula

Two successive discounts $d_1$ and $d_2$ are equivalent to a single discount of:

$D_{\text{eq}} = d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{100}\%$

*Example:* Discounts of 20% and 10% give $20 + 10 - 2 = 28\%$ (NOT 30%!).

Equal Selling Price Rule

When two articles are sold at the same SP, one at $x\%$ gain and the other at $x\%$ loss:

$\text{Net Result} = \text{Always a Loss of } \left(\frac{x}{10}\right)^2\% = \frac{x^2}{100}\%$

*Warning:* If they had the same Cost Price, the result would be 0%. But at same Selling Price, it is always a loss!
