Profit, Loss & Marked Price

Three prices, three different bases. Markup % is computed on CP. Discount % is computed on MP. Profit % is computed on CP again. Every trap in this chapter comes from mixing them up.

See the price waterfall

The bar grows by markup (on CP), then shrinks by discount (on MP). Where it lands relative to CP is the net profit or loss.

Equal selling price — why it's always a loss

Same +x% and −x%, same SP. Two different CPs. Always a loss of (x/10)²%. Move the slider to see why.

Loss %1%
CP of gain item₹900
CP of loss item₹1,100
Formula(x/10)²

When two articles are sold at the same SP with ±x%, the total is always a loss of (x/10)²%. If they had the same CP, the answer would be 0%. The base swap is what creates the loss.
