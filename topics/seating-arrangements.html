Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall. Read after Practice — then race the timed set.

  • Use

    Fix one seat

    Pin one person, then place relative clues. Facing flips left/right.

  • Trap

    In vs out

    Centre: left = CW (this lab). Outward reverses. Linear south also reverses.

  • Check

    Opposite

    Even n: opposite = +n/2 seats. Odd n: no true opposite.

  • Speed

    Negative clue

    "Not adjacent" removes a set of seats — don't lock one seat too early.
