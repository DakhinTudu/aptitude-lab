2. Predict Before You Reveal

Avoid the #1 Doubling Trap: Linear addition vs. Exponential doubling.

Challenge: The Eight-Fold Multiplication Puzzle

A sum of money doubles itself in 5 years under compound interest (compounded annually).
In how many years will this sum become 8 times of itself?

Mathematical Resolution:

Correct Answer: 15 years.

In Compound Interest, money multiplies by the same geometric factor over equal intervals of time:
• At $T = 0$: Amount $= 1P$
• At $T = 5$ years: Amount doubles $\implies 2P$
• At $T = 10$ years: Doubles again $\implies 2 \times 2P = 4P$
• At $T = 15$ years: Doubles again $\implies 2 \times 4P = \mathbf{8P}$!

If a sum becomes k-fold in T years, it becomes kᵐ-fold in (m × T) years.

Why 35 is wrong: 35 years would be the correct answer if this were Simple Interest where each 5-year block adds $+1P$ of interest linearly. In compound interest, it only takes 15 years!

3. Essential CI & SI Formulas & Exam Shortcuts

Axiomatic formulas that resolve 3-year differences and EMI calculations instantly.

1. The 2-Year Difference Formula

The difference between CI and SI for 2 years at annual rate $R\%$:

Δ₂ = CI₂ - SI₂ = P × (R / 100)²

To find Principal: $P = \Delta_2 \times (100 / R)^2$.

2. The 3-Year Difference Shortcut

Difference for 3 years:

Δ₃ = P × (R / 100)² × (300 + R) / 100

Ratio of 3-year gap to 2-year gap: $\frac{\Delta_3}{\Delta_2} = \frac{300 + R}{100} = 3 + \frac{R}{100}$.

3. Compounding Frequency Shift

When interest is compounded $k$ times a year:

  • Half-Yearly ($k=2$): Rate $= R/2\%$, Periods $= 2T$.
  • Quarterly ($k=4$): Rate $= R/4\%$, Periods $= 4T$.
  • Effective Rate: $\text{EAR} = (1 + R/100k)^k - 1$.

4. Equal Annual Installments (Loans)

Loan amount $P$ repaid in 2 equal annual installments of ₹$x$ at compound rate $R\%$:

P = x / (1 + R/100) + x / (1 + R/100)²

⚠ Never divide total amount by 2. Future money must be discounted to present value.
