Averages, Mixtures & Alligation

Alligation is a balance beam. The mixture mean sits where the two masses balance. Most traps come from putting the wrong values on the beam — especially a selling price against cost prices. This page makes the lever, the weighted-average pull, and the same-basis trap all visible.

Understand: replace one value — why the average moves

Don't memorise a formula. One number leaves, one enters. Total changes by (new − old). Average moves by that ÷ n. See the slide — then the mind shortcut is obvious.

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall. Read after Practice — then race the timed set.

  • Use

    Alligation cross

    |c₂−mean| : |mean−c₁| = quantity ratio. Mean must lie between the two prices.

  • Trap

    Weighted ≠ simple

    Don't average averages unless group sizes are equal.

  • Check

    Replacement

    New avg = old ± (swap-in − swap-out)/n.

  • Speed

    Dilution

    Left fraction = (1 − removed/total)^k after k equal removals.
