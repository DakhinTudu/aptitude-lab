Module 3 · Average speed

Average speed — equal distance vs equal time

Equal distance → harmonic mean 2uv/(u+v). Equal time → arithmetic mean (u+v)/2. They only match when u = v.

True average40 km/h
Naive (u+v)/245 km/h
Harmonic 2uv/(u+v)40 km/h
Gap naive − true5 km/h
Trap: most candidates answer the arithmetic mean. For equal distances, the harmonic mean is correct — the slower leg takes more time, so the average is pulled toward it.

Module 9 · Commercial mixtures

Adulteration, profit through mixture, ratio changes

Four families — formula cards you can verify mentally in the exam hall.

Adulteration

Cheaper substance mixed with expensive, sold at pure price. Profit% = (cheap/substitute ratio × 100).

Profit% = (milk/water) × 100

Test: milk:water 5:1 sold at milk price → profit 20%.

Profit through mixture

Cost from mixing, SP, profit%. Reverse: find SP from CP, or CP from SP + profit.

CP = weighted avg; Profit% = (SP−CP)/CP × 100

Test: mix 2 kg @ ₹100 + 3 kg @ ₹150 → CP ₹130. Sell @ ₹150 → 15.4% profit.

Mixing to reach target %

Combine a low-% solution with a high-% solution to reach a middle target.

Test: 20% and 50% to reach 30% → 2:1.
