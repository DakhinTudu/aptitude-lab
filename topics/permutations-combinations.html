Permutations, Combinations & Counting Principles

Explore fundamental counting principles, order-dependent arrangements ($^n P_r$), unordered group selections ($^n C_r$), repeated-letter word permutations, string/block vs. gap methods, and geometric combinatorics.

The Golden Decision Rule: Ask yourself: Does the order matter? If yes (arranging letters, podium finishes, secret PIN codes), use Permutations ($^n P_r$). If no (selecting committees, choosing pizza toppings, shaking hands), use Combinations ($^n C_r$).

Foundational Combinatorial Invariants

1. Arrangements vs. Selections
  • Permutation ($^n P_r$): $\frac{n!}{(n - r)!}$ (Order matters).
  • Combination ($^n C_r$): $\frac{n!}{r!(n - r)!} = \frac{^n P_r}{r!}$ (Divide by $r!$ to remove order).
  • Symmetry: $^n C_r = ^n C_{n-r}$ and $^n C_0 = ^n C_n = 1$.
  • Pascal's Identity: $^n C_r + ^n C_{r-1} = ^{n+1} C_r$.
2. Restricted Arrangements
  • Repeated Items: $\frac{n!}{p_1! \cdot p_2! \cdots p_k!}$ for identical duplicates.
  • Block Method ("Always Together"): Treat constrained items as 1 composite unit. Multiply by internal permutations.
  • Gap Method ("Never Together"): Arrange unconstrained items ($k!$), then place constrained items in $(k+1)$ gaps.
  • Circular Table: $(n - 1)!$. Necklace / Garland: $\frac{(n - 1)!}{2}$.
3. Geometric Combinatorics
  • Handshakes among $n$ people: $^n C_2 = \frac{n(n - 1)}{2}$.
  • Diagonals of $n$-sided polygon: $^n C_2 - n = \frac{n(n - 3)}{2}$.
  • Triangles from $n$ points ($m$ collinear): $^n C_3 - ^m C_3$.
  • At least 1 item chosen from $n$: $2^n - 1$.
