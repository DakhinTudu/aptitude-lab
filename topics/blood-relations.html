3. Core Exam Rules & Tree Conventions

Standard symbols, generational tiers, and the cardinal rule of gender.

The Cardinal Rule of Gender

Never assume gender from a name or sibling link!

  • "A is the brother of B" ⟹ A is definitely Male $[+]$, but B's gender is Unknown $[?]$.
  • Unless B is stated as male or female, B can be brother OR sister.
  • If asked "How is B related to A?", the answer is Cannot be determined!

"Only Child / Daughter / Son"

Possessive resolution depends on the speaker's gender:

  • Woman: "Only daughter of my mother" ⟹ Herself.
  • Man: "Only daughter of my mother" ⟹ His Sister.
  • Man: "Only son of my father" ⟹ Himself.
  • Woman: "Only son of my father" ⟹ Her Brother.

Standard Symbolic Tree Notation

  • Male: Square $[+]$ (Blue)
  • Female: Circle $[-]$ (Purple)
  • Married Couple: Double line ($A = B$)
  • Siblings: Single horizontal line ($A - B$)
  • Parent to Child: Vertical branch ($\mid$)
