⚠️ Common trap:

Why rates add (and times don't)

Two streams merge into one bigger stream. The merged flow rate is the sum of the two — that's why together finishes sooner.

A's rate + B's rate = combined rate. Since the combined rate is larger than either alone, the combined time is smaller. Time is inversely proportional to rate — that's the whole chapter in one sentence.

Wage sharing — the trap most people miss

When workers share wages, the split is by daily rate, not by days. Drag the wage pool to see the difference.

Understand: leftover work — rates, not days

Hard questions look scary until you see: finish some work alone → shorter job left → both attack the leftover with rates. Never add “A's days + B's days”.

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall. Read after Practice — then race the timed set.

  • Use

    Rates add

    1/A + 1/B = 1/together. LCM of days = total work units.

  • Trap

    Never average days

    (12+18)/2 is not together-time. Harmonic: 2/(1/12+1/18) if equal share only for two.

  • Check

    Pipes

    Fill positive, empty negative. Net rate → time = 1/|net|.

  • Speed

    Efficiency ratio

    If A:B = 2:1 and together = 12d, A alone = 18d (A does ⅔ per day-block).
