Time, Work & Efficiency

Rates add. Times never do. Two workers together are always faster than the fastest one alone. This page covers 11 modules — combined work, efficiency, alternate days, men/women/children, pipes & cisterns, leakage, wages, and 34 practice questions.

Step-by-step solution

⚠️ Common trap:

Why rates add (and times don't)

Two streams merge into one bigger stream. The merged flow rate is the sum of the two — that's why together finishes sooner.

A's rate + B's rate = combined rate. Since the combined rate is larger than either alone, the combined time is smaller. Time is inversely proportional to rate — that's the whole chapter in one sentence.

Alternate-day work simulator

A works on day 1, B on day 2, A on day 3, and so on. See the cumulative work and the exact day the job finishes.

Total work30
A's rate3 u/d
B's rate2 u/d
Completed on6 d
Work left0
How to solve
See the pattern. A does 3 units on day 1, B does 2 units on day 2, so a 2-day cycle = 5 units. Total work = 30 units → 6 cycles = 12 days? But the work finishes mid-cycle when the leftover is smaller than a full day's rate.
Key idea
Find cycle work first. Then see how many full cycles fit. The last cycle may finish mid-day — and that's where most students lose marks.
Who starts matters
If A starts, day 1 is A's rate; if B starts, day 1 is B's rate. Different totals, different final answer.
Last day is partial
The last worker only works for a fraction of the day. Time = full days + fraction. Report "completed on day N" or the fractional day.

Pipes & cisterns simulator

Inlet pipes have positive flow, outlet pipes have negative flow. Net rate determines the actual fill time.

Inlet rate+0.25/h
Outlet rate0/h
Net rate+0.25/h
Fill time4 h
ResultFills
Net rate formula
R_net = Σ R_inlet − Σ R_outlet. Time = 1 / R_net. If R_net is negative, the tank never fills (drains faster than fills).

Leakage simulator

A pipe fills in T₁ hours alone, but with a leak it takes T₂ hours. How long would the leak alone take to empty the full tank?

T₂ must be > T₁ (leak slows it).
Pipe rate+1/6
Net rate (with leak)+1/8
Leak rate−1/24
Leak alone empties24 h
Derivation
Net rate = 1/T₂ − 1/T₁. The leak rate is negative: |leak| = 1/T₁ − 1/T₂. So the leak alone empties the tank in T₁T₂/(T₂−T₁).
Shortcut formula
Leak time = T₁ × T₂ / (T₂ − T₁)
Pipe fills alone in T₁; with leak in T₂. The leak alone would empty in T₁T₂/(T₂−T₁) hours.

Workforce simulator — men, women, children

Convert everyone to the same efficiency unit, then add. A man-day, woman-day, and child-day are different sizes — never mix them without converting.

48 man-days
Men contribution100%
Women contribution0%
Children contribution0%
Combined (man-equiv)4
Days to finish12 d
Conversion method
4 men × 12 days = 48 man-days. Convert all workers to man-equivalents: men + women/1.5 + children/2. Then days = 48 / combined.

Men-days-hours relationship

When the work is the same, M₁D₁H₁ = M₂D₂H₂ (assuming equal efficiency per hour). With different efficiencies, add E as a factor.

4 days
Formula
M₁D₁H₁ = M₂D₂H₂ → D₂ = (M₁ × D₁ × H₁) / (M₂ × H₂). Use this when total work is unchanged.
With efficiency
M₁D₁H₁E₁ = M₂D₂H₂E₂
If a worker in case 2 is 50% more efficient, use E₂ = 1.5. The efficiency is now a part of the product.

Efficiency comparison lab

If A is X% more efficient than B, A's time is LESS than B's. The direction of the conversion trips people up.

A is 50% more efficient than B.
A's efficiency1.5
B's efficiency1.0
A's time12 d
Efficiency ratio (A:B)3 : 2
Time ratio (A:B)2 : 3
Key relationship
If A is 50% more efficient: A's efficiency = 1.5 × B's. A's time = B's time / 1.5 = 2/3 of B's time. Efficiency ratio 3:2 → time ratio 2:3. Notice they're INVERTED.
⚠️ The direction trap: "A is 50% more efficient" means A does 1.5× the work per day, so A takes 1/1.5 = 0.667× the time. Students who add or subtract percentages from time get it backwards.

Understand: leftover work — rates, not days

Finish some work alone → shorter job left → both attack the leftover with rates. Never add "A's days + B's days".

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall.

Rates add
1/A + 1/B = 1/together. LCM of days = total work units.
Never average days
(12+18)/2 is not together-time. Together must beat the fastest worker.
Pipes — signs matter
Inlet = +, outlet = −. Net rate → time = 1/|net|.
Leak formula
Leak time = T₁T₂/(T₂−T₁). Pipe alone T₁, with leak T₂.
Efficiency ratio
More efficient = fewer days. Efficiency ratio n:m → time ratio m:n. Inverted.
Men-days-hours
M₁D₁H₁ = M₂D₂H₂ (same work, same efficiency). Add E when efficiency differs.
Wages
Split by rate (work done), not by days. Wage ratio = efficiency ratio = inverse of time ratio.
Alternate days
Cycle work = sum of rates for the cycle. Full cycles first, then the partial last day.
Workforce conversion
Convert everyone to man-equivalents: M + W/(men:women) + C/(men:children). Then work / rate.
