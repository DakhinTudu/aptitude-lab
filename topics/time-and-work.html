3. Core Exam Rules & Fast Shortcuts

Mathematical principles, unit work rules, and condition boundaries.

The LCM (Unit Work) Method

Avoid fractions by setting Total Work equal to the LCM of individual days:

  • Total Work $W = \text{LCM}(D_A, D_B)$ units
  • Daily Rate of A = $W / D_A$ units/day
  • Daily Rate of B = $W / D_B$ units/day
  • Combined Days = $W / (u_A + u_B)$

The Product-Over-Sum Shortcut

For exactly two workers working together throughout:

$T_{\text{together}} = \frac{D_A \times D_B}{D_A + D_B} \text{ days}$

*Limitation:* Do NOT extend this formula to 3 workers as $\frac{a \times b \times c}{a + b + c}$ — that is mathematically false! For 3 workers, use the LCM method.

Work, Efficiency & Wages Rule

When workers work for the same number of days, wages are shared in the ratio of their daily efficiency (rates), NOT their days:

$\text{Wage}_A : \text{Wage}_B = \text{Rate}_A : \text{Rate}_B = D_B : D_A$

*Warning:* Faster workers take fewer days but do more work per day!
