3. Pattern Signatures & Exam Recognition

How to spot the pattern family within 5 seconds.

Double Differences (Quadratic)

If the first differences ($D_1$) grow steadily, check the second difference ($D_2$):

  • $D_1$: $+4, +6, +8, +10 \implies D_2: +2$ (constant)
  • Formula type: $a n^2 + b n + c$
  • Next $D_1 = 10 + 2 = 12 \implies$ Next term $= 31 + 12 = 43$.

The Half-Step Multiplier

When a series halves first and then increases steadily:

$8 \xrightarrow{\times 0.5} 4 \xrightarrow{\times 1} 4 \xrightarrow{\times 1.5} 6 \xrightarrow{\times 2} 12 \xrightarrow{\times 2.5} 30 \xrightarrow{\times 3} 90$

*Signature:* $A \to A/2 \to A/2 \to 1.5A$. Multiplier increases by $+0.5$ each time.

Prime Difference vs. Odd Numbers

If differences are $+2, +3, +5, +7\dots$, beware!

  • If the first difference is $+2$ (even), it is a PRIME sequence!
  • The next prime after $7$ is $+11$, NOT $+9$.
  • Odd numbers would be: $+1, +3, +5, +7, +9$.
