Module 7 · Advanced applications

Distribution, ratio changes, product changes, points vs %

Four families that test whether you understand what "of" anchors to.

Percentage distribution: when a total (money, employees, population) is divided into shares, each share = share% × total. Reverse: share% = share / total × 100.
Percentage points vs percentage change: interest goes from 10% to 12%. That is 2 percentage points (a subtraction) but a 20% relative change ((12−10)/10 = 0.20). Both are "correct" — the question decides which you report.
Percentage change in product: if length +a% and breadth +b%, area multiplier = (1 + a/100)(1 + b/100). So +20% and −10% gives 1.20 × 0.90 = 1.08 → area up 8%. The trap is adding +20 and −10 for "+10%".

Exam shortcuts

Speed card for the exam hall.

Use

Multiplier, not ±%

+20% → ×1.20 · −20% → ×0.80 · successive: multiply multipliers.

Trap

Base-shift

"50% more than B" ≠ "B is 50% less than A". Less = gap ÷ new base.

Reverse

Original = New / multiplier

Don't subtract p%. Divide by the multiplier to undo the change.

Note

Points vs %

10% → 12% is +2 points, but +20% relative. The question decides.
