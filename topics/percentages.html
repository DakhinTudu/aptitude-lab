⚠️ Common trap:

Base-shift — the #1 percentage trap

"A is x% more than B" does not mean "B is x% less than A." Slide and see why.

A vs B125
B vs A100
B is less than A by20%

Formula: % less = x / (100 + x) × 100. This is why a 25% price rise needs only a 20% cut in consumption to keep spend unchanged.
